# Exact short cycles inside the fixed native corpus

This new library enumerates merge3 identities (two factors shared), flip4 identities (one factor shared), and six telescoping plus5 coordinate paths. Every endpoint and derived point must already be an atom in the authenticated dictionary. It introduces no atoms and does not relax native source eligibility. Reverse endpoint order is covered by reversed coordinate paths; shared-factor flips try both orders of the remaining axes.

The catalogue is complete only for these listed algebraic families when every loop finishes within its caps. It is not a catalogue of all zero subsets of support<=5. Each accepted mask is deduplicated and checked against the complete regenerated tensor columns; the exact GF2 cycle-span rank is frozen. Limits/cancellation yield UNKNOWN with explicit completed families, cursor, observed counts and no negative/global-minimum claim. The deterministic process uses no RNG and does not claim resumability.

Default caps are2,000,000 pair/path trials and10,000 distinct cycles. Full-column regeneration and the last arithmetic/callback operation have cancellation barriers. The production native input is pinned by prepared raw SHA and source-manifest raw SHA; the unchanged native contract reconstructs all producer/root/export provenance and all36 full tensor source checks before dispatch.

An optional one-hop scan crosses each original verified seed with every catalogue mask. It computes `delta=weight(cycle)-2*popcount(seed&cycle)`. Every negative event constructs a candidate; repeated identical candidate states reuse their first proof location. Candidates are never fed back into the seed set. Evaluation/candidate/time/output caps remain explicit and partial scans remain UNKNOWN. No all-codeword minimum is inferred.

Production seed scanning requires a separate independently pinned QA receipt binding prepared SHA, source-manifest SHA, sparse-core SHA, runtime SHA and full runtime-closure SHA. Enumeration-only mode does not authorize scanning. Direct cached guard09ac provides fresh stages with <=0.1-second inner polling, while clocks/signals remain checked throughout. CPU0/nice10/1024MiB/120inner seconds and32MiB output apply under a150-second outer limiter.

Negative candidates enter hidden `.raw-candidates/` with nested UNKNOWN envelopes. Complete tensor syndrome, primary exhaustive checker, then independent499 exhaustive checker must all pass. Fresh global/resource/clock and code/input pins guard publication. A target<=92 is frozen under `first-record-evidence`; the global operations guard owns global record freezing/stopping. Any late/error phase after a move, scan receipt or final receipt quarantines locally published candidates and marks UNKNOWN or FAILED_CLOSED. Raw catalogue/scan data remain hidden and readable after cutoff. All final receipts, including apparent winners, pass a final forced barrier.

Tiny tests include all three exact families, missing-point rejection, bounded/late completion semantics, known zero-padding reduction, both checker gates, and actual main()/final-cutoff quarantine. They do not perform production enumeration or optimization. The known tiny reduction is not a novelty claim.
