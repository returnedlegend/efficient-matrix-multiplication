# Nine-case binary packet pilot: independent QA pass

The exact nine prepared mixed15/d11 packets passed source, compact-core,
controller-bridge and publication-lifecycle QA. Clearance is limited to one
fixed-policy heuristic attempt per packet, target14, CPU1 nice10/1024MiB,
120 seconds overall,12 per case and150 outer. No trained policy is promoted;
UNKNOWN closes no mathematical case. Independent QA made no N5 optimizer call.

The original native catalog had full core hashes for only three of the nine
deferred packets. An append-only query-free receipt now pins all nine actual

initial14-slot approximation drops a minimum-volume source product; its loss
matches that product's exact compact volume.

An intermediate custom runner leaked its visible candidate directory when a
cutoff occurred between verified-record publication and its separate freeze
marker. The independent failing control is preserved in
`binary-packet-late-freeze-repro-v1.*`. The final runtime removes that duplicate
publication protocol. It calls the unchanged reviewed mixed12 `run_case`, with
its50ms inner CancelPoller, forced stage barriers, persisted primary and
independent whole-tensor validation, canonical records directory, and late/error
quarantine. No local WINNER or `first-record-evidence` runtime publication
remains; the canonical campaign guard owns global freeze.

The controller bridge accepts only shape11 cubed/target14, uses the fixed
policy with training disabled, and preserves the full target. Only
RAW_EXACT_COMPACT with separately checked packed and scalar zero residual maps
to SAT/products. Every other observation maps to UNKNOWN. Full global
replacement and eligibility remain the reviewed lifecycle's responsibility.

Four independent final-bridge tests plus five adapter and five controller
tests passed **14/14**, zero failures/errors/skips. They include an actual N2
whole-tensor positive with64 primary and64 independent coefficient checks;
