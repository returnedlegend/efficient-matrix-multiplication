Let a finite binary tensor be viewed along one free axis as raw matrices M_j in V = Mat(a,b; F2). Let S = span{M_j} and d = dim S. The raw list may contain zero, repeated or dependent matrices. Its CP rank is the minimum number of nonzero rank-one matrices whose linear span contains S: a CP decomposition gives these opposite-factor matrices; conversely solve each M_j in such a span and use those coefficients as the free-factor vectors. Thus ordinary flattening gives rank at least d. This statement is about the actual slice image, not the number of listed free slices or source products.

Define B0 as the span of every rank-one matrix in S. For each rank-two D in S, enumerate its three unordered decompositions D=P+Q into nonzero rank-one matrices. Since D is in S, P and Q have the same quotient class q in V/S. Ignore q=0, whose endpoints are already in B0. For a nonzero class q, let E_q be all D obtained in this way. Then

    CP rank <= d+1  iff  B0=S or some nonzero q satisfies span(B0,E_q)=S.

For necessity, take a minimum rank-one support and let L be its span. Its points are linearly independent: any dependent point can be eliminated while adjusting the free-factor coefficients, reducing the number of products. Hence dim L is its support size and S is contained in L. If the size is at most d+1, dim(L/S) is at most one. If it is zero, the rank-one support is inside S and B0=S. Otherwise choose an outside support point P0. All outside support points P_i have the same nonzero class q, and all inside points lie in B0. Replacing every P_i by P_i+P0 shows that S is spanned by the inside points and the star differences P_i+P0. Each nonzero difference has matrix rank at most two. A rank-one difference belongs to B0; a rank-two difference is enumerated and its original endpoints have quotient q. Therefore B0 and E_q span S. No combination of distinct nonzero classes is used.

For sufficiency, if B0=S, choose a basis consisting of original rank-one points in S, giving d products. Otherwise collect original rank-one points spanning B0 and original endpoints of all the edges in one successful nonzero class q. Their span L contains every D in E_q, hence S. All collected points lie in S+span{P0} for any one collected outside endpoint P0. Consequently dim L<=d+1. Choose an independent basis from these original rank-one points, solve every raw M_j against it, and assemble the free coefficients. This gives at most d+1 products and restores the complete tensor. A basis chosen from the original points is essential: echelon XOR combinations need not remain rank-one. Zero free coefficients may remove unused products, and tensor verification/canonicalization must still gate any full-algorithm export.

Exactly three unordered decompositions hold over F2. Fix D=u1*v1^T+u2*v2^T with both u and v pairs independent. Every two-point decomposition has the same two-dimensional column and row spaces, so its ordered factorization differs by one of the six matrices of GL(2,F2). Swapping the two terms divides this count by two. Explicit representatives are {u1*v1^T,u2*v2^T}, {(u1+u2)*v1^T,u2*(v1+v2)^T}, and {u1*(v1+v2)^T,(u1+u2)*v2^T}. There is no nontrivial scaling over F2. The lemma is not an unrestricted-field claim.

An implementation can therefore scan only the 2^d matrices in S, compute their ranks, retain original inside rank-one points, and factor each rank-two matrix three ways. Quotient reduction and small span calculations group the resulting edges. It need not enumerate every pair of ambient input forms. The relevant scan parameter is d, so an exponential 2^d bound must remain explicit. A stopped, capped, malformed or incomplete enumeration is UNKNOWN. A complete failed criterion excludes only CP<=d+1 for this exact compressed residual and selected axis, not an unrelated tensor family or global matrix-multiplication rank.

Edge cases: d=0 is the zero tensor with rank0. If the desired bound is below d, flattening excludes it. A desired bound equal to d uses only B0=S; the outside-coset success has rank d+1. The theorem does not decide bounds above d+1. Rank-one D need not have its many two-term decompositions enumerated, because it already belongs to B0. Rank-two edges with q=0 are redundant. All raw free slices must be restored, including dependent and zero slices, before expanding compressed factor coordinates back to the ambient tensor and checking unchanged outside products.
