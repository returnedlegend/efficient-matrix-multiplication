# Exact even flip-span components

This new controller separates disjoint supports of the frozen sparse flip4 catalogue. Small components are minimized by exhaustive Gray enumeration with local-coset caching. Larger components use a separately controlled exact XOR/cardinality solver: local atom-selection variables are affine XORs of an independent selector basis, with a PySAT sequential at-most counter and single-thread CryptoMiniSat. Each local proof has a feasible state of weight k and UNSAT below k; UNKNOWN keeps the last feasible state and safe lower bound. Spawned children are polled, cancelled, and reaped. The portfolio permits at most21 distinct large local cosets, at most60 aggregate child CPU seconds (a59-second dispatch budget plus1-second overhead reserve), and120 wall seconds including authentication. Native backend and cardinality dependency bytes are pinned and frozen. The objective is additive across disjoint supports plus fixed atoms. Results concern only the selected starting cosets; they do not bound other code cosets or the global tensor rank.

Inner generators are only the authenticated even flip4 cycles. Current immutable inputs yield seven distinct flip-span cosets, with three added starts of weights96,96,98.

It reauthenticates the frozen native corpus, validates every catalogue identity/syndrome, binds runtime and input raw hashes at publication/final barriers, and quarantines eligible candidates if later guards fail. Unfinished components report UNKNOWN and retain valid bounds and feasible states; no optimality follows from a timeout.
